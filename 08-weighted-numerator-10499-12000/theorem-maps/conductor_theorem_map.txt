Radical conductor and actual reflected numerator bridge
======================================================

File: WeightedQRH/RowConductor.lean
Namespace: WeightedQRH.RowConductor

Upstream input is Detector/RowRadicalConductor.lean, already present with a
compiled .olean in the source distribution (unchanged relative to fork commit
c388e88e6, whose retained declarations come from openai/math fd4aeeb2e). In particular,
rawRow_small_presentation constructs a smaller modulus with ALL element
coefficients preserved, using explicit radical periodicity of the local
sextic row character and the fixed 72/m bad-prime factors.

exists_row_small_presentation S hS u extends this to the actual excluded
physical row rowCharacter S hS u. It yields τ such that:
  N(modulus τ) <= fixedConductorConstant S * N(radical(span u));
  elementCoeff(rowCharacter S hS u,n) = elementCoeff(τ,n) for EVERY n.
This includes zeros on ramified/deleted primes, not merely agreement away
from a finite set. The fixedConductorConstant is the existing upstream one.

idealCoeff_eq_of_elementCoeff_eq and inverse_elementCoeff_eq carry this
identity to all ideals and inverse characters. polynomial_eq_of_elementCoeff_eq
therefore identifies every actual smoothed polynomial, for either plain or
Möbius coefficients, arbitrary profiles and shifts. The original row/bin is
not replaced by an unrelated character.

principal_iff_of_equal_elements preserves nonprincipality.
LFunction_eq_of_elementCoeff_eq gives exact L-function equality for Re(s)>0,
s!=1, hence preserves all zero-bin data in the region used here.

row_original_split S hS u hn returns τ, ψ, g, where ψ is primitive,
nonprincipal, |g|=1, and the actual physical polynomial equals
  g * (retainedSchwartz τ ψ + discardedSchwartz τ ψ).
These are the upstream retained/discarded reflection expressions, with
τ.inverse agreeing with the original physical row.inverse on ALL ideals.

For C_u = N(modulus ψ) * N(redundantIdeal(modulus τ,modulus ψ)), the same
chosen ψ satisfies C_u <= N(modulus τ) <= C_S N(radical(span u)).
For ANY injective selected prime tuple P_i and exponents e_i>=2 with
P_i^e_i | span u, the chosen C_u simultaneously satisfies
  C_u * product_i N(P_i)^(e_i-1) <= C_S N(span u).
The quantifiers are important: one ψ and one g work for all selected tuples.
reflected_scale_selected_deficit_real is the real-valued convenience form;
reflected_scale_pos proves positivity of this exact combined scale.

This combined scale includes the entire redundant Euler product. Every
subset used in the reflection has no larger product norm, and the smooth
ideal denominator only decreases the reflected scale. Consequently one
need not assert that redundant primes belong to a fixed set. Their
coefficient-mass bounds remain the reflection-module responsibility.

These are structural conductor/reflection lemmas. They do not themselves
prove the new weighted moment estimate or the final QRH theorem.
