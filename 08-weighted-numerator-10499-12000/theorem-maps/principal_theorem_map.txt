Principal finite-product, contour, and normalization formalization
==============================================================

All files below are in WeightedQRH/. They use the upstream (unchanged relative to
fork commit c388e88e6, whose retained declarations come from openai/math fd4aeeb2e)
localMultiplier, slotMultiplier, windowMultiplier, continuedSourceMultiplier,
sourceMultiplier, sourceResidueIntegral, principalRowIntegral, and Hecke signal
objects. The proofs are parameter extensions of the corresponding upstream
Detector modules; they do not postulate an arithmetic/probe obligation.

PrincipalRemainderRegion.lean (namespace WeightedQRH): remainder_* lemmas
extend the actual Euler correction to x.re >= theta, w.re >= 9/10,
z.re >= 4/25, retaining the summable 240 Q^(-17/10) defect bound.
The broader region provides analytic neighborhoods of the actual integration
lines w=19/20 and z=33/200. It imports the original CorrectionTail structure.

PrincipalRemainderFiniteProduct.lean (namespace PrincipalFiniteProduct):
local/slot/combined analyticity and norm bounds. In particular
combined_slot_bound_sharp gives the original finite multiplier with the
new local error factor 1+1440 Pmin^(-theta). No multiplier is redefined.

PrincipalRemainderFiniteProductX.lean (namespace PrincipalFiniteProductX):
continued_source_analytic_x and continued_source_differentiable_strip hold
on x.re > max(theta, HeckeZeroSupremum.beta).

PrincipalRemainderContours.lean (namespace PrincipalContours):
continued_source_joint_integrable, continued_residue_pair_integrable,
source_ordered_outer and supporting Fubini/profile lemmas at a>=theta.
These are actual complex integrals, not abstract numerical majorants.

PrincipalRemainderTransport.lean (namespace PrincipalTransport):
source_initial_ordered_at_a and source_initial_joint_ordered_at_a move the
actual initial source contours to a>theta, then take the w/z residues.
Analyticity, growth, horizontal boundary decay, and Fubini are discharged
in the proof. The continued reciprocal handles the possible exceptional
real points of the original raw reciprocal, with equality almost everywhere.

PrincipalRemainderBounds.lean (namespace PrincipalRemainderBounds):
window_sharp_bound and ordered_remainders_scale_bound give actual integral
bounds. source_scale_identity uses sum_j slotEll_j=ell, X=Z^lx,Y=Z^ly:
  scalePower * product_j (Z^slotEll_j)^xi
  = Z^(a-(4+b)/6 + h*(xi-1/6) + ly*(upsilon-1)).
source_w_scale_identity: exponent a-(4+b)/6+h*e-ly/20.
source_z_scale_identity: exponent a-(4+b)/6-h/600.
source_remainders_strict_saving: for 0<e<=1/1000 and
beta+e in [theta,2], both principal remainder integrals are bounded by
C/e or C times Z^(beta-(4+b)/6-1/4000). The explicit saving is 1/4000.

PrincipalRemainderPhysical.lean (namespace PrincipalPhysical):
principal_physical_pool_ordered identifies the actual finite tuple sum of
principalRowIntegral with these transported source integrals, assuming
pairwise distinct chosen primes. This identity is valid for arbitrary
positive X,Y,Z and a>theta.

PrincipalRemainderPhysicalBounds.lean (namespace PrincipalPhysical):
physical_principal_residue_remainder bounds the actual weighted finite
principal tuple sum minus sourceResidueIntegral by
C/e * Z^(beta-(4+b)/6-1/4000), at X=Z^lx,Y=Z^ly and total length ell.

PrincipalRemainderResidueActual.lean (namespace PrincipalResidueActual):
normalized_actual_window_residue applies PrincipalSignal's compiled theorem
to the same original windowMultiplier and original residue weights, with
normalizer sourceResidueConstant * principalScalar(total length ell).
The error is D*Z^(a+signalShift)*P^(-theta).

PrincipalRemainderRayThresholds.lean (namespace PrincipalRaySlots):
power_pool_thresholds replaces the old 7/8 power in the small local-error
condition by theta. Ray class mass/normalizer facts are unchanged upstream
statements, valid for arbitrary positive slot lengths.

PrincipalRemainderNormalized.lean (namespace PrincipalNormalized):
actual_ray_principal_comparison gives the final actual normalized principal
pool sum versus HeckeSignal.signal, with error at most
 C [Z^(beta-(4+b)/6-1/4000+nu)
    + Z^(beta-(4+b)/6+e-theta*ellMin)]
eventually in Z. It assumes smooth nonnegative nonzero prime weights,
positive distinct slot lengths summing to ell, positive ellMin, and the
standard upstream SourceExclusions/ray-class hypotheses. These hypotheses
are explicit; this theorem does not construct their common probe instance.

Scope: these modules certify the principal branch only. They do not claim
end-to-end QRH or the independently new weighted numerator branch.
