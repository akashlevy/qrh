Scope limitation
----------------
This certifies the analytic moment/count input, not the new complete nonvanishing
argument. The weighted reflected-numerator coefficient expansion, Holder step,
dyadic error assembly, geometry selection and common-probe closure are separate
modules being assembled by the other agents. The numerical exponent improvement
is not certified as a complete QRH theorem until those actual objects connect.
