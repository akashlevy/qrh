Audit: NumeratorSourceBin and NumeratorSourceClass (2026-10-09)

Verdict: the compiled source packet/class interfaces preserve the required
uniformity and actual rowwise choices. No additional analytic premise or
vacuous beta>7/8 assumption occurs. The retained finite count and physical
amplitude/geometry premises still need actual source-cell specialization.

Order of choices:
- Fixed D, F, beta>=51/100, source fine mesh and error mask J precede the
  existence of degree, positive C and the eventual U threshold.
- Every U above that threshold allows arbitrary Z,d with U=Z^d, all physical
  target characters eta, retained contours x,z and height, finite physical rows,
  actual full amplitude bin, R and Ccount.
- The moment used by SourceBin is uniform over all rowwise LabelPacketIndex
  choices, all coefficient boxes and selected subsets. It is an actual original
  physical polynomial moment, not a profile-dependent coefficient shortcut.
- The per-row reflection data are chosen once from the proved actual
  nonprincipal reflection existence theorem, and the same combined conductor
  enters the packet mass and moment dilation.
- Moment constants do not depend on Ccount or on any of those rowwise choices.
  The class count enters only the final Holder/count stage.

Actual scales and masks:
- Y=U^(ly/d). The support cutoff is N<=2Y, since F.W is supported in [1,2].
- Fine source widths D.ell_j/d satisfy the certified moment mesh by the fixed
  weighted_detectorMesh/200 choice and d>=1/2.
- Original source prime lengths Z^(D.ell_j) equal U^(D.ell_j/d) exactly.
  The explicit product dictionary also resolves the dependent selected subtype.
- SourceBin uses box width=annular slack=extra moment loss=t. Its resulting
  fourth moment exponent is 1+2t.
- SourceClass chooses common selected subsets for each box at v=j*t+2t.
  This exactly equals selectedCapacity(zbase-4t/9)(j*t+t); both small length
  deductions are present. If capacity is exhausted, strict positivity of the
  source widths forces selected empty and the certified zero-slot moment applies.
- Error slots have zero gain in q but all slot widths stay in its denominator.
  The actual full amplitude class is never replaced by a main-slot projection.
  Its explicit count premise remains the count of that same full class.
- The selected squared spike is square-rooted with positive U. The actual
  selected canonical product has the proved crude bound <=U. The main-product
  upper slack t*ell/d is divided out before the packet bound and exactly restored
  afterward; it is visible in the final exponent.

Constants:
- SourceBin sets Cr'=max(1,Cr); Cmoment=Cr'*(1+height)^degree is >=1.
  The fixed coefficient-box count ceil(1/t)+1 is absorbed into C.
- The coefficient packet mass has loss t, the numerator fourth moment has 2t,
  and selected-slot mesh loss is delta*weighted_detectorMesh(t)/2 before the
  Holder half-power. The final class exponent records each contribution.
- All finite error masks can be collected with NumeratorFiniteHeight without
  choosing the contour height first. It supplies a common real degree and
  positive constant, including a bound for the entire sum of height terms.

Empty-family cases:
- SourceClass explicitly assumes the actual amplitude class is nonempty only to
  read its amplitude-bin labels. An empty class contributes zero. The separate
  reconstructedDyadValue_class_uniform theorem already handles that case.
- SourceBin/SourceClass require a prime-label choice Pbase for the error subset J.
  For J=empty this is the unique empty tuple; no nonexistent prime is requested.
  If a physical prime pool is empty, the original full tuple numerator is zero;
  integratedDyadValue_zero_of_no_labels proves this separately. The final finite
  collection must perform that split or obtain a full tuple witness first.
- An empty selected family is allowed and controlled by the actual ZeroAt input.
- The moment finite row set may be empty: all sums vanish and all rowwise choice
  types are harmless. SourceClass's nonempty premise is used only for selection,
  not to disguise a missing moment for actual nonempty classes.
