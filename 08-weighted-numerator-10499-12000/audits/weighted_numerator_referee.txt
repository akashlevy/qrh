SECOND AUDIT OF THE ALL-ERROR WEIGHTED NUMERATOR ARGUMENT
2026-10-09, independent check against original paper78.tex local identities.

Reviewed artifact: research_audit/independent_count.txt, sections 2--4.
Conclusion: its all-error extension is valid subject to the original plain
moment and reflection inputs, with the exact support simplification below.
This audit is analytical, not Lean kernel verification.

A. Quotient-free decomposition.

For each error prime, write E_p=G_p+conjugate(chi_p(u))*H_p, including
chi_p(u)=0 on ramified primes. The correction term with error index set I
is exactly a sum over its prime labels of product p^(z-1)E_p times the
product H_p outside these labels, times the main-slot polynomials outside
I. The source's disjoint underlying slot supports are essential here.
This identity follows directly by expanding G_p=-conj(chi_p(u))H_p+E_p.
No local quotient or reciprocal H_p is required.

B. Coefficient norms, not just values.

The local P_p^* formula is linear in T=(Np)^(-w). Consequently H_p is
quadratic in T for p not dividing u, and linear for p dividing u. G_p
and E_p are also polynomials of uniformly bounded degree. Denominators
1-R,1-V,1-D are independent of w. At an unramified prime, first simplify
P_p^*+D exactly, before taking coefficient absolute values. In particular,
its constant coefficient is

 [R(1-Q^(-1))/(1-V)-D R]/(1-R),

not the sum of the absolute values of -D/(1-R) and D. This cancellation
is within one coefficient. After this simplification the source defect
proof bounds the polynomial coefficient l1 norm at each real w line.
With rho=delta/4 and sigma=1/2-rho, all unramified error exponents are
at most -.51 (or smaller) uniformly for a>=.51 and z_r=.34. The usual
O(P_i) count of available prime ideals therefore gives a slot norm at
most P_i^(z_r-1/2), with spare negative power.

C. Explicit ramified exponent check.

For p dividing u, put j=v_p(u), 1<=j<=5. The identity

 G_p=bar(eta(p))*Q^s*(1-V)*P_p^* - Q^(-w)*H_p

is exact since D=W=0 at this prime. Include its slot scalar Q^(z-1),
and divide by the reference Q^(z_r-1/2). At s_r=a=(1+delta)/2 and
w_r=1/2-rho, the J_j boundary terms have real exponents:

 j=1: -1+rho;
 j=2: -delta;
 j=3: -delta+rho, and -3delta/2;
 j=4: -3delta/2+rho;
 j=5: -5delta/2.

These follow by direct substitution into the original six-row J_j table.
They are all negative for rho=delta/4. Increasing s_r to a+16e only
improves them. The R/(1-V) tail has exponent
3.5-5s_r-6z_r <=-1.09. The extra -Q^(-w)H_p term has exponent
-1+rho and bounded local coefficient norm. The strict second-family
term for j>=2 is the only exception:

  -eta(p)*(Q-1)*Q^(-s-w)/(1-R),

which after the stated normalization has coefficient norm at most
Q^rho. For j<=1 it has an additional V, hence no exception.
At w_r=1/2, its cost is at most one.

D. Conductor allocation is spent once.

For any term after triangle-expanding the finite product of local
polynomials, let J be exactly the strict ramified labels in that term.
The labels are distinct. The original primitive conductor assertion gives

 C_u <= C_S rad(u)
     <= C'_S U product_{p in J}(Np)^(-(j_p-1)).

Since each strict term has j_p>=2,

 (C_u/(C'_S U))^rho product_{p in J}(Np)^rho <=1.

This applies once to the entire term, and thus also after summing terms.
Other ramified choices cost only a divisor bound. Unramified choices
cost the already counted P_i prime supply. Therefore if
F_I(w)=sum_k A_{I,u}(k)(Nk)^(-w) and
B_I=product_{i in I}P_i^(z_r-1/2), then both

 sum_k |A_{I,u}(k)|(Nk)^(-1/2) << U^epsilon B_I,
 (C_u/(C'_S U))^rho
   sum_k |A_{I,u}(k)|(Nk)^(-1/2+rho) << U^epsilon B_I

hold with all constants uniform in row phases and external heights.
Combining these two (using max(1,x)^rho<=1+x^rho) yields the required
weighted coefficient norm. It does not require any uniform global norm
sum_k sup_u |A_{I,u}(k)|, which would be unjustified.

E. Exact support closes the scale-range issue.

The exact Mellin reconstruction before reflection contains
S_chi(Y/Nk;W1), and W1 is compactly supported in (0,infinity).
If its upper support endpoint is B, this expression vanishes unless
Nk<=B Y, since every integral ideal n has norm at least one.
TRUNCATE THIS EXACT SUM BEFORE APPLYING REFLECTION.
Then

 T=max(1,C_u Nk/(C'_S U)) <= max(1,BY),

because C_u<=C'_S U. Thus v=log_U T lies in a fixed bounded interval
0<=v<=y+O(1/log U). The main reflected length is at most
1-y+v+o(1)<=1+o(1). All moment and profile parameter ranges are
therefore bounded as required by the original source. The remaining
annular tail of the reflected profile is handled by the source's usual
fixed-tolerance annular truncation. No theorem uniform in unbounded
log-length is needed. Any discussion of reflecting arbitrarily long
T dyads back, or choosing a new rho for their tail, can be deleted.

F. Character and mask compatibility.

Physical sixth-power-free rows have each good conductor prime exactly
once and no redundant moving good puncture. Fixed S omissions can be
restored as in the source natural reflection formula, with fixed scale
multipliers and subpower/bounded coefficient mass. The reflected plain
sum has row character conjugate(chi_u), matching the physical main
prime factors. Conjugating the whole moment returns the positive
orientation in the stated plain theorem. Underlying prime supports are
still disjoint and every original nonunit zero remains zero. Any physical
row at the large scales used here ramifies outside S, hence its inducing
character is outside the finite fixed family Theta.

G. Holder and capacity.

At scale T=U^v the reflected log-length is at most n0+v+o(1), n0=1-y.
The selected capacity decreases by at most v/(3kappa). The fourth
moment supplies a gain q*z(v)/2 in the first absolute moment, while
the coefficient weight supplies rho*v. Since

 rho-q/(6kappa) >=5delta/36>0

for kappa>=3/4 and q<=delta/2, every scale dyad is controlled by the
v=0 exponent. The full main prime product is still paid at U^(qL).
No selected prime is removed a second time. Error slots have amplitude
zero; selecting all positive slots when supply runs out still gives
at least the requested q*z gain by the original greedy selection lemma.

H. Integration strategy caution.

The new argument integrates w before amplitude subdivision in the
remaining external parameters. No row set depending on a moving contour
parameter may simply be analytically continued as a separate function.
The conservative comparison of the old and new full-bin bounds is
min(max_q E_old,max_q E_weighted). A stronger pointwise-in-q comparison
requires explicitly noting that the amplitude set is independent of w,
and making both w bounds after all contour moves in s,z for the ORIGINAL
fixed dynamic-bin sum. The conservative comparison avoids this extra
bookkeeping and is sufficient if its exact exponent certificate passes.

This audit found no additional analytic hypothesis beyond the original
moment, reflection and local-identity package. A complete improved
zero-free theorem still requires its exact global exponent certificate,
low estimate, outer-row bounds, principal remainder and continuation
checks; this document alone does not certify those other pieces.
