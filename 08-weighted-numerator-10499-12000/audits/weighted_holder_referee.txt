Independent referee note: weighted numerator and selected-prime Holder
9 October 2026. Author: gram_audit subagent.

This note audits the main (no local-error slot) numerator replacement
and its exponent certificate. It does not by itself certify the error
slots, principal residue, or complete new theorem. Those require their
separate proofs. No feedback reduction of kappa is used here.

1. The weighted Holder step

Fix a dynamic zero bin delta, physical Mellin parameters s,z, an
amplitude vector (g_i), and one row set C of cardinality at most U^(R+e).
Let q be the mean prime amplitude. Select a fixed subcollection of the
positive-amplitude slots, of total requested length z_sel, as in the
original selection argument. Its spike is

  |Q_sel(u)| >= U^(q z_sel - e)  for u in C.

The original plain fourth moment, with two copies of S and kappa=3/4,
gives

  sum_rows |S(u)|^4 |Q_sel(u)|^2 << U^(1+e)

provided 2n+6 kappa z_sel <= 1 (with the usual small strict reserve).
Holder with powers 4 and 4/3 therefore gives

  sum_(u in C) |S(u)|
   <= (sum_C |S(u)|^4 |Q_sel(u)|^2)^(1/4)
      (sum_C |Q_sel(u)|^(-2/3))^(3/4)
   << U^((1+3R)/4 - q z_sel/2 + O(e)).

It is legal to reuse prime slots already employed to prove the row
cardinality. These are simultaneous inequalities about the same set;
neither argument requires probabilistic independence or disjointness
between the two uses. Within each amplitude vector the selected subset
is fixed, independently of the individual row.

2. Row-dependent Euler coefficients: obstacle and repair

A rowwise coefficient-mass estimate is NOT enough for a direct exchange
of the Euler expansion with a fourth-moment row sum: the coefficients
depend on u, particularly at p|u. A common coefficient majorant was not
established. The following supremum method avoids that exchange.

Write H_u(s,w,z)=sum_k h_k(u;s,z) Nk^(-w), with s,z fixed. For

  a=(1+delta)/2,  Re s=a+16e,  Re z=17/50,
  rho=delta/4,   sigma=1/2-rho,

the coefficient l1 norm satisfies

  sum_k |h_k(u;s,z)| Nk^(-1/2+rho) << U^e.

Reason: the original local proof may be conducted in the coefficient
l1 algebra at w=sigma. The denominators R,V,D are w-independent. The
already-cancelled defect formula (paper78.tex equation local-H-defect)
is polynomial in p^(-w), with the original convergent w-independent
geometric denominators. Apply its triangle inequalities termwise in
that algebra. The good-prime defect is O(p^(-1-c)) and the ramified
defect O(p^(-c)) for a fixed c>0, because

  Re s+sigma = 1+delta/4+16e,

uniformly inside local region one for delta>=1/50. The good-prime
positive product converges; the product over p|u is U^e by the source
divisor-product estimate. No common coefficient majorant is needed.

Start w on an absolute line, expand H and L, and invert the W1 Mellin
transform. For the centrally normalized plain sum
S_chi(X)=X^(-1/2)sum_n chi(n)W1(Nn/X), the exact main reconstruction is

  (1/(2pi i)) integral Y^(w-1) W1hat(w) H_u(w)L_u(w) dw
   = Y^(-1/2) sum_k h_k(u) Nk^(-1/2) S_chi(Y/Nk).

All rows under consideration are nonprincipal. Analytic continuation
and the source all-height bounds identify this with the w=1/2 integral;
the discarded w tails are negligible after choosing sufficient decay.
Compact support of W1 implies the summand vanishes unless Nk<<Y.
In particular the auxiliary scales occupy a bounded logarithmic range
when d is bounded below.

By the coefficient-mass bound, the reconstruction is at most

  Y^(-1/2) U^e sup_k [Nk^(-rho) |S_chi(Y/Nk)|].

For Nk about U^v, use functional-equation reflection and then the
source's scale Sobolev method. The original moment proof already
performs coefficient mass -> rowwise scale supremum -> Sobolev for
conductor-dependent scales (paper78.tex lines12735--12822). The
primitive conductor times the natural redundant radical is O(U), so
every retained reflected length is at most

  n(v)=max(1-ly/d+v,0)+arbitrarily small loss.

Dyadic boxes in the bounded logarithmic scale range cost only U^e.
Differentiating the scale changes only the smooth profile, leaving the
row character and selected prime product unchanged. The primitive root
number has modulus one. The source reflection restores the natural
zero extensions, so no new row-dependent coefficient class is inserted
into the plain moment.

For d>=1/2>ly, the baseline n=1-ly/d is positive and below 1/2 in the
proposed geometry. Its selected capacity is

  z_sel(0)=(2ly/d-1)/(6 kappa).

At v>0, decrease the capacity to
max(z_sel(0)-v/(3 kappa),0). Relative to the v=0 bound this loses at
most qv/(6 kappa) after Holder. The coefficient weight gains rho v.
Since q<=delta/2,

  rho - q/(6 kappa)
   >= delta(1/4 - 1/(12 kappa)) = 5delta/36 > 0

at kappa=3/4. Therefore v=0 supplies a valid exponent bound after the
sum or supremum over v. For zero remaining capacity, the zero-slot
plain moment has no length restriction. This covers the rest of the
v range as well.

3. Contour and amplitude quantifiers

Physical main factors Q_i(u;z_phys) do not depend on w. Consequently
they can be retained in the amplitude classification while w is
inverted. This assertion alone does not cover the local-error factors.

A contour branch may depend on the fixed dynamic bin delta. It must
not be chosen separately for pointwise amplitude sets inside that bin.
The proposed certificate obeys this requirement: it chooses the old
branch for delta<=1/3 and the weighted reflected branch for delta>=1/3,
then bounds all x=q/delta in [0,1/2] within the chosen branch.
The original full bin is continued before those amplitude subdivisions.

4. Independently rerun exact arithmetic

The file weighted_numerator_certificate.py was rerun with its output
stored as weighted_numerator_gram_referee.json. It passed:

  theta=10499/12000, ell=167/1000, b=43/500,
  lx=747/2000, ly=919/2000, h=1587/2000,
  kappa=3/4, margin=1/10000,
  1 old-branch and 9 weighted-branch Bernstein rectangles,
  32 auxiliary rational checks.

I independently matched the polynomial numerator with the direct
exponent expressions. At row norm U=Z^d the replacement of the old
high exponent is

  d[(1-R)/4-delta/2] + ly delta/2
     -q(2ly-d)/(12 kappa).

The displayed polynomial uses d=h, as required. Its common denominator
J is certified positive on the full rectangle. The branch choice is by
delta only. The Bernstein conversion and rectangular subdivision use
exact Fraction arithmetic.

For d in [1/2,h], prime supply z_sel(d)<=ell/d is equivalent to
ell>=(2ly-d)/(6 kappa), whose strongest endpoint is d=1/2. This is
exactly one of the checked rational inequalities. The weighted slope
in d is (1+3R)/4-17/50+q/(12 kappa), positive because R>=1-delta
and delta<=3/4. The maximum therefore occurs at h. A sufficiently
small extension above h is allowed by the strict endpoint margin.

As a negative control, raising the claimed uniform margin from 1/10000
to 1/1000 was correctly rejected at delta=7/16, x=1/2.

5. Current referee verdict

The main reconstruction, weighted Holder extraction, capacity loss,
row-dependent scale handling, and exact exponent certificate have no
unresolved blocker identified in this review. The coefficient-mass
supremum repair is essential; omitting it would leave a real gap.

This is not yet a verdict on the complete theorem. In particular, the
full local-error product must be reconstructed with equally strong
weighted control, and the complete contour/residue proof must combine
that error estimate with this main estimate. The amplitude assignment
g_i=0 on error slots cannot on its own supply the needed numerator
fourth moment for their w-dependent factors.

6. Subsequent adversarial review of the complete local-error lemma

I subsequently read weighted_numerator_lemma.tex in full. Its weighted
coefficient-norm argument resolves the local-error issue just identified.
The following are independently checked points, rather than an appeal
to its author's conclusion.

(a) Quotient-free expansion. Define E_p=G_p+bar(chi_p(u))H_p. In a term
of the selected-tuple expansion with error slots I, every main-selected
prime contributes -bar(chi_p(u))H_p. Its H_p rejoins the unselected
product. Thus the Euler product excludes precisely the error labels,
and every remaining main slot is the original w-independent Q_i(u;z).
This proves the claimed F_I identity without dividing by H_p or P_p.

(b) Good-prime coefficient estimates. At w=1/2-rho, the local error's
four exponents are -a, -6z0, 4-5a-6z0, and 1-(1/2-rho)-6z0, up to
favorable e terms. For a>=.51, delta<=.75, rho=delta/4 and z0=.34,
each is strictly below -1/2. Summing the slot's O(P_i) labels with
their P_i^(z0-1) normalizer gives at most P_i^(z0-1/2).

(c) Ramified coefficient estimates. After dividing by P_i^(z0-1/2),
the non-strict boundary exponents at e=0 recompute to

  j=1: -1+rho;
  j=2: -delta;
  j=3: -delta+rho and -3delta/2;
  j=4: -3delta/2+rho;
  j=5: -5delta/2.

All are negative. The R,V tails and the explicit -Q^(-w)H_p term are
smaller. The sole strict j>=2 term has normalized cost Q^rho on the
left coefficient line and at most 1 on the central line.

(d) One conductor deficit for the entire tuple. At a good prime of a
physical row, the primitive conductor exponent is exactly one. If J
is the set of strict ramified error labels, disjointness of slot
supports therefore gives

  C_u <= C_* U product_(p in J) Q^(-(j_p-1)).

Hence (C_u/(C_*U))^rho product_J Q^rho <=1. This is applied once to
the whole tuple before summing the divisor-many labels. It does not
reuse a single conductor saving for several different slot primes.

(e) Resulting coefficient mass. If F_I=sum_k A_(I,u)(k)Nk^(-w) and
B_I=product_(i in I)P_i^(z0-1/2), the two local coefficient bounds give

  sum_k |A_(I,u)(k)|Nk^(-1/2) << U^e B_I,
  (C_u/(C_*U))^rho
  sum_k |A_(I,u)(k)|Nk^(-1/2+rho) << U^e B_I.

Adding them bounds the same sum weighted by
max(1,C_u Nk/(C_*U))^rho. This is exactly the weight needed after
reflection, whose scale is C_u Nk/Y. Thus the row-dependent
coefficient supremum loses no power from a strict ramified error.

(f) Mask restoration. All good primes dividing the physical u already
have conductor exponent one, so additional redundant factors of this
particular numerator are supported in fixed S. Their reflection
coefficient mass remains summable with rho<1/2. They create only fixed
constants and the same permitted scale suprema. This is stronger than
the generic masked-row situation and justifies using C_u, rather than
a larger moving redundant conductor, in the weighted mass.

Current verdict after this additional review: the complete new weighted
numerator lemma, including every local-error subset, has no unresolved
analytic blocker identified here, conditional only on its expressly
imported original analytic lemmas. This is an AI-assisted mathematical
review, not human peer review or Lean verification. The complete theorem still needs the parent
proof to assemble its contour, residue, low-side and count interfaces;
the lemma and numerical certificate alone are not that assembly.
