INDEPENDENT WEIGHTED PHYSICAL NUMERATOR AUDIT
2026-10-09. Sources: original local paper78.tex only; no Li Xiang proof.
Scope: proves a main-slot weighted-numerator estimate from the original
fourth-moment lemma and explicit original Euler identities. It does not by
itself establish the full modified-probe theorem: error-slot subsets,
outer row ranges, low geometry and principal continuation remain required.

1. PRECISE CLAIM

Let U=Z^d, Y=Z^ly=U^y, 1/2 <= y <= 1. Work in a fixed dynamic
bin a=(1+delta)/2 with delta>=1/50, and on one main-slot amplitude
set C with #C << U^(R+epsilon). Fix the external s,z parameters,
Re(s)=a+16e, Re(z)=17/50. Write L=ell/d for the total slot length.
Assume 3/4<=kappa<=1 and beta_* <=(1+kappa)/2 whenever kappa<1.
The physical rows u are sixth-power-free, prime to the fixed set S,
and induce outside the fixed ray family Theta. All slots retain their
original disjoint prime supports and zero extensions. Let

  n0=1-y,   z0=min(L,(1-2n0)/(6kappa)),   0<=q<=delta/2.

For the term in which every slot is its main prime sum, the full w
integral (including the ORIGINAL Euler correction H_u) satisfies the
following row-sum bound, with fixed seminorm/height factors understood:

  sum_{u in C} | (1/2pi i) int Y^(w-1) W1hat(w)
                       L^S(w,chi_u) H_u(s,w,z) dw |
                   product_i |Q_i(u;z)|
  << Y^(-1/2) U^((3R+1)/4+qL-q z0/2+epsilon).

There is the usual arbitrarily small capacity/mesh loss. The row set C
may depend on the retained s,z parameters; no separate meromorphic
continuation of C is needed. The assertion is applied pointwise in those
parameters after moving the original fixed dynamic-bin sum.

2. EXACT w-COEFFICIENT STRUCTURE

Paper78 lines 4010-4057 gives, with T=Q^(-w),

 P_p^*=(1-Rp)^(-1) [ Rp(1-Q^(-1))/(1-V)
          -eta(p)(Q-1) Q^(-s) T V^(1_{j<=1})/(1-V) + J_j(T) ],

where every J_j is a polynomial of degree <=1 in T; Rp and V are
independent of w. Its normalized H_p is a polynomial of degree <=2
in T. At a ramified prime j>0, W=D=0, so its degree is <=1.
Consequently there are exact coefficients h_k(u;s,z), indexed by
integral ideals k with local exponents at most two, for which

 H_u(s,w,z)=sum_k h_k(u;s,z) (Nk)^(-w).

These coefficients depend on the varying row. They must NOT be inserted
as arbitrary coefficients in the fourth-moment theorem. The following
rowwise coefficient norm eliminates them first.

Set rho=delta/4 and w0=1/2-rho. Then

 s_r+w0 = 1+delta/4+16e >1,
 w0>=1/4 (if delta<=1),
 s_r>=51/100,
 z_r=17/50.

At p not dividing u, put E_p=P_p^*+D. The cancellation of the
constant coefficient is exact before taking coefficient absolute values:

 E_p= [ Rp(1-Q^(-1))/(1-V) -D Rp
        +T {chi_p(u) Rp-eta(p)(Q-1)Q^(-s)V/(1-V)} ]/(1-Rp).

The original defect identity is

 H_p-1=[D(V+W-VW)-VW+(1-V)(1-W)E_p]/(1-D).

Taking the weighted l1 norm of this POLYNOMIAL in T, at |T|=Q^(-w0),
therefore has exactly the same monomial upper bounds as the source's
local-defect proof, without relying on cancellation between distinct
powers of T. In particular

 |h_{p,0}-1| + |h_{p,1}| Q^(-w0)+|h_{p,2}| Q^(-2w0)
    << Q^(-1-epsilon_H)             (p not dividing u),
 |h_{p,0}-1| + |h_{p,1}| Q^(-w0)
    << Q^(-epsilon_H)               (p dividing u),

where one may reduce epsilon_H to min(delta/4,1/50); a further fixed
factor reduction safely covers a bounded positive e range. The strict
ramified term has exponent 1-s_r-w0=-delta/4-16e; the other ramified
terms are smaller by the source's six-valuation table. All unit phases
are bounded uniformly. Hence, by multiplication and the divisor-product
bound,

  sum_k |h_k(u;s,z)| (Nk)^(-1/2+rho) <<_epsilon U^epsilon.       (A)

This bound is uniform in all imaginary parts of s,z and in the physical
row; constants may depend on fixed bin buffers and arithmetic data.
The fact that ramified local majorants are not summable over ALL primes
is harmless: only primes dividing this row u occur in that finite product.
One should not replace (A) by sum_k sup_u |h_k(u)|..., which is stronger
and is not proved.

3. EXACT NUMERATOR RECONSTRUCTION AND POSITIVE ENLARGEMENT

Define S_u(X)=X^(-1/2) sum_n chi_n(u) W1(Nn/X), with the original S
exclusions. On an absolute w line, Mellin inversion and the preceding
Euler series give

 B_u := (1/2pi i) int Y^(w-1) W1hat(w)L^S(w,chi_u)H_u(s,w,z) dw
      = Y^(-1/2) sum_k h_k(u;s,z)(Nk)^(-1/2) S_u(Y/Nk).        (B)

The sign of the scale shift is Y/Nk, not Y Nk. W1 has compact support
in (0,infinity), so in (B) only Nk<=C_W Y can occur. Since the
numerator is nonprincipal and H is holomorphic from an absolute line
to w0, the same identity represents the shifted w integral. Horizontal
joins are justified by the original polynomial strip bounds and arbitrary
Mellin decay. The denominator L^S(s,eta conjugate(chi_u)) is independent
of w and remains outside this inversion.

By (A),

 |B_u| <= Y^(-1/2) U^epsilon
           sup_{1<=K<=C_W Y} K^(-rho) |S_u(Y/K)|.             (C)

This is the key legitimate positive enlargement: all row-dependent
arithmetic coefficients have disappeared into a uniform scalar. What
remains is only a rowwise SCALE supremum in an allowed plain polynomial,
not a rowwise coefficient choice. A logarithmic scale partition plus the
parameter Sobolev lemma bounds the supremum by finitely many profile
derivative integrals, costing U^epsilon. The K range is polynomial, so
there are O(log U) unit boxes in log K.

4. REFLECTION, CONDUCTOR, AND MASKS

For a physical sixth-power-free u, every good prime dividing u has
primitive conductor exponent exactly one (paper78 lines 8960-9010).
There is no additional moving redundant prime mask: the character is
already zero precisely at those good conductor primes, together with
fixed S. Its primitive conductor scale C_u satisfies C_u<<_S U.
The fixed S omissions may be dealt with by the source's natural
reflection identity, with bounded or subpower total coefficient mass.

At K=U^v, the original plain scale is X=Y/K=U^(y-v). Reflection
therefore produces scales <=U^(1-y+v+xi)=U^(n0+v+xi), with the
conjugate natural row character. This inequality remains useful even
when the actual conductor is much smaller than U. Small actual
conductor only makes the reflected scales shorter. Coefficient mass,
subunit scales, large transformed annuli and row-dependent scales are
handled exactly as in paper78 lines 12720-12820: summable annular
weights followed by a rowwise scale supremum and Sobolev. Errors can
be made an arbitrary negative power, uniformly after finite derivative
orders are fixed. A possible root number has modulus one and is removed
only in the absolute value.

The reflected numerator has character conjugate(chi_u), the SAME
orientation as each physical main slot Q_i. Conjugating the entire
product gives positive orientation chi_u, which is the orientation of
Lemma plain. Every Q_i coefficient is 1_{p in 1_T}, a fixed finite
combination of the allowed ray characters; no growing conductor is
hidden in it. Common masks and original nonunit zeros remain intact.
Row restrictions may be removed from the resulting nonnegative moment.
Physical large rows ramify outside S, so they induce outside Theta,
as required by the positive-slot case.

5. WEIGHTED HOLDER ESTIMATE

For a reflected scale of length n<=n0+v+xi, select whole positive slots
of total length <=

 z(v)=min(L, max(0,(1-2(n0+v))/(6kappa)))

with a small capacity decrement and mesh loss. The original amplitude
selection proof gives |Qsel|^2>=U^(2q z(v)-epsilon).
The original fourth moment, with two copies of the same reflected plain
polynomial and the selected slots, gives

 sum_u |S_n(u)|^4 |Qsel(u)|^2 << U^(1+epsilon).

Holder on the particular amplitude set C then gives

 sum_C |S_n(u)|
   <= U^(-q z(v)/2+epsilon) (#C)^(3/4)
             (sum_u |S_n(u)|^4 |Qsel(u)|^2)^(1/4)
   << U^((3R+1)/4-q z(v)/2+epsilon).

The full main-slot product is <=U^(qL+epsilon). Importantly, the slots
selected to prove the moment are NOT additionally removed from that
physical product; the gain has already come from their lower bound
inside the fourth moment. This avoids either missing or double-counting
a half-amplitude factor.

Since

 z(v)>=max(0,z0-v/(3kappa)),
 rho=delta/4 > q/(6kappa)       (q<=delta/2, kappa>=3/4),

we obtain for every v>=0

 -rho v -q z(v)/2 <= -q z0/2.

In fact rho-q/(6kappa)>=5delta/36 when kappa>=3/4.
Insert this uniform inequality into (C), use the scale-Sobolev bound,
and sum the logarithmic scale boxes. This proves the claim in Section1.
The zero-capacity branch uses the unrestricted zero-slot fourth moment;
there is no division by a vanished prime factor.

6. CORRECTION TO THE HIGH EXPONENT

The old all-main estimate bounds the w part rowwise by
Y^(-a) U^(R+delta/2), apart from the same main-slot product U^(qL).
The new estimate replaces that part by
Y^(-1/2) U^((3R+1)/4-qz0/2).
Thus the base-Z change to the OLD high exponent is exactly

  ly*delta/2 + d*((1-R)/4-delta/2-qz0/2).

For a reference theta, the resulting all-main high exponent is

 E_weighted = a-theta + h*(17/50-1/6) -ly/2-ell/2+qell
              +d*((3R+1)/4-17/50-qz0/2).

It is valid to take the minimum of this and the old all-main estimate.
It is NOT yet valid to apply this formula to every error-slot subset
without an additional analysis of its w-dependent factors and ramified
conductor savings. That separate issue is material for a full proof.

7. AUDIT STATUS

Established analytically from the explicit original identities and the
original plain-moment theorem: the entire all-main weighted-numerator
route, including row-dependent Euler coefficients and conductor scales.
This is a derived lemma, not a kernel-checked Lean theorem. It does not
claim an unconditional improved theta until the other physical-probe
pieces and global exponent optimization have been proved.
