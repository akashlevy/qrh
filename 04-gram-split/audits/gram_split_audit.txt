Independent audit of the Gram-split route, 9 October 2026

Scope
-----
This audit concerns gram_split/note/theta_split_note.tex and its stated
dependencies. It does not claim a new zero-free half-plane theorem. The
underlying 7/8 manuscript is formalize_11_12/papers/paper78.tex.

1. The claimed 0.8665 improvement is not a proved theorem
-------------------------------------------------------
The final note explicitly makes its improvement conditional on a bound for
the theta pairing. Earlier working files that claim 0.8665, 0.871 or 0.864
are superseded by their own retraction. Their old Voronoi input controls
rho(m), whereas the actual reflected pairing involves rho(m) zeta_m.
The remaining functional-equation/Gram claims also still depend on an
unwritten general-cusp constant-term calculation. Calling these claims
"proved modulo routine lemmas" does not turn them into an unconditional
proof. In particular an exact exponent certificate only establishes the
consequence of its analytic inputs.

2. Independent algebra check of the missing reflection scalar
-------------------------------------------------------------
The source manuscript's eq. reflection has a scalar zeta outside the
dual sum; eq. reflection-row-phase gives its moving-prime dependence.
For a squarefree good row m, every moving exponent is j_p=1, hence
the dual column factor is chi_p(x)^(-3)=chi_p(x)^3, a quadratic symbol.
However the row scalar does not disappear.

Ignoring fixed ray factors only (which can be tracked without altering
the following conclusion), the local factor is

  tau^-_{p,1} tau^+_{p,3} = -alpha(p) conjugate(chi_p(4))
                                      conjugate(gamma_2(p)).

Indeed tau^-_{p,1}=chi_p(-1) gamma_1(p), tau^+_{p,3}=gamma_3(p),
gamma_1(p) gamma_2(p)=-alpha(p) conjugate(chi_p(4)) gamma_3(p),
and gamma_3(p)^2=chi_p(-1). These are precisely the prime-Gauss and
signal identities in the source.

The reflection cross-prime factor is

  product_{p|m} chi_p(m/p)^4.

Twisted multiplicativity gives

  product_{p|m} conjugate(gamma_2(p))
   = conjugate(gamma_2(m))
     product_{p<q, p q|m} [chi_p(q) chi_q(p)]^2.

The two cross-prime factors cancel because their product has exponent
six. Including the outer conjugate(alpha(m))^2 therefore yields

  zeta_m = (fixed ray factor) mu(m) conjugate(alpha(m))
                                          conjugate(gamma_2(m))
         = (fixed ray factor) conjugate(gamma_2(m))^4,

using gamma_2(m)^3=mu(m) alpha(m). The fixed ray factor includes the
fixed branch amplitude; it need not have modulus one before normalizing
that branch. The m-dependent Gauss factor is the essential issue.

Thus the relevant coefficients are pi(m)=rho(m) zeta_m. A theorem for
quadratic twists of rho does not, merely by its statement, give the
corresponding theorem for pi. This is an unsupported implication, not
a proof that the desired estimate for pi is false.

3. A precise obstruction for all arguments using only these two norms
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Write the unweighted form for clarity; bounded positive weights and
unit phases can be incorporated into the vectors. The available sizes are

  ||rho||_2^2 <= Q^(5/6+epsilon),   ||B||_2^2 <= Q^(1+epsilon).

They imply |sum_m rho(m) B_m| <= Q^(11/12+epsilon). They cannot imply
any uniform power saving on their own. To see this, take any unit vector
u and set rho=Q^(5/12) u and B=Q^(1/2) conjugate(u). Both norm bounds
hold with equality, while the pairing equals Q^(11/12). This is a
sharp logical obstruction for norm-only arguments; it does not assert
that the actual arithmetic B has this adversarial alignment.

After multiplication by the probe prefactor Q^(-1/2) Y^(-1/3), the
theta contribution is bounded at the scale

  Q^(5/12) Y^(-1/3)
    = X^(1/2) (Y^2/Q)^(1/12),    Q=XY.

Squaring the excess gives exactly the old P_a^(1/6) Gram loss.
Therefore splitting out rho and applying Cauchy-Schwarz to it again
does not remove that loss. Removing P_a^2/Y alone cannot improve a
parameter optimum where that term is inactive.

4. Exact 5/6 barrier for the residual-Cauchy-Schwarz template
----------------------------------------------------------
This is a limited no-go theorem, proved directly from the source
normalizations. It continues to apply even if a future estimate makes
the theta pairing arbitrarily small.

Assumptions defining the template:
  (i) The high-side signal has exponent
      C(s)=s+l_x/2-1+h/6,
      h=1-l_x+ell, as in source eq. stage-mellin-exponent.
  (ii) l_x>ell>=0, so every rescaled X'=Z^(l_x-d), 0<=d<=ell,
       grows as a positive power.
  (iii) The residual contribution is estimated only by
      ||A_rest||_2^2 <= (Q/Y) Z^epsilon,
      ||B||_2^2 <= Q Z^epsilon,
      followed by Cauchy-Schwarz and triangle inequalities.
  (iv) The unrescaled block is retained (d=0), as in the given
       compensated probe, and the available block estimates are used
       separately, without cancellation between residual blocks.

The resulting estimate is |I_rest| <= X^(1/2) Z^epsilon. In the
general loss notation it is Z^(l_x/2+(g+e)/2+epsilon), with g,e>=0.
To use this estimate uniformly for zeros arbitrarily close to a
claimed boundary sigma_0, the low-side contract requires

  l_x/2+(g+e)/2 <= C(sigma_0),

equivalently

  sigma_0 >= 1-h/6+(g+e)/2
           = 5/6+(l_x-ell)/6+(g+e)/2
           > 5/6.

This also follows by taking d=0 in the exact checker, whose other
blocks have an additional -d. The contract allows omega>0, but that
does not bypass this floor: omega must be smaller than beta_*-sigma_0,
and beta_* may be arbitrarily close to sigma_0. The supplied upper
bound must consequently work with arbitrarily small exponent slack.

Even allowing l_x-ell to tend to zero gives infimum 5/6, still far
above 1/2. The necessary way around this obstruction is to change
at least one defining assumption: prove cancellation in the residual
pairing or between blocks, change the row moment scale, change the
signal geometry, or use a different probe. This theorem does NOT
prove that every future development of the metaplectic method fails.

5. The weight-cancellation obstruction is exact within its stated scope
---------------------------------------------------------------------
For fixed Q and a fixed arithmetic probe, put

  K(x)=integral W_1(t) t^(1/6) W_0(x/t) dt/t.

Mellin convolution gives Khat(s)=W_1hat(s+1/6) W_0hat(s), hence

  Khat(5/6)=W_1hat(1) W_0hat(5/6).

The right side is the high-side signal coefficient. Thus a finite
linear combination that makes the combined K vanish identically
also makes the combined signal coefficient vanish. This proves the
note's stated result. It does not classify all Q-dependent weights,
all arithmetic changes of probe, or cancellation estimates that do
not make K identically zero.

6. Status of the analytic split
-------------------------------
The contour-shift implication itself is correct, conditional on the
stated analytic information about

  D_c(w;m)=sum_s c(s) g_tilde(s,-m) N(s)^(-w).

If its only possible pole in Re(w)>1/2 is the simple theta pole 2/3,
and if a mean square of size Q^(1+epsilon) holds on 1/2+epsilon with
polynomial vertical growth, then Mellin inversion and a contour shift
give

  A_sig(m)=Y^(-1/3) W_1hat(1/6+iv) rho_c(m),
  sum_m Omega |A-A_sig|^2 <= (Q/Y) Z^epsilon.

The file wf/FE_LoA6_proof.md supplies a substantial proof framework,
but its general-cusp constant terms, isotypic transfer, and finite-order
input are not fully written out. The first of these is substantive:
it supplies the continued scattering matrix and control on the far
left line needed for the proposed approximate functional equation.
I found no reason to call this mean square false, but the supplied
note is not a complete proof of it. None of this closes the pi-pairing
gap even if all these tasks are finished.

7. Public improvement and novelty boundary
-----------------------------------------
Independently checked primary source on 9 October 2026:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12234
Baiying Liu, "Slightly improved zero-free half-planes for the
quasi-Riemann hypothesis", submitted 8 October 2026.

Its abstract states both 34999/40000=0.874975 and
(1507-2 sqrt(921))/1653=0.874957069799..., together with a Lean
formalization claim. The presence of the paper and the statements
are verified. Their full analytic proof and Lean dependency closure
are separate audits, not established merely from this abstract.
Consequently reproducing one of these bounds is not a new improvement
over the public record, although it does satisfy theta<7/8 literally.

8. Audit evidence
-----------------
Inspected the source manuscript's continuation-criterion,
stage-mellin-exponent, prime-Gauss identities, signal identity,
reflection, reflection-row-phase, reflection-cross-prime-phase,
and its completed-row and Gram norm application.
Inspected the final note, REPORT, AUDIT_chain, FE_LoA6_proof,
PARTII_slotfree, D_derivation, theta_split_model and exact_cert_theta.

New verification: research_audit/check_conditional_slotfree.py is an
independent pure-rational certificate for the note's conditional
sigma_0=1733/2000 geometry. It uses the exact zero-slot row count and
reduces each continuous high-side inequality to a concave quadratic,
so it needs neither floating-point optimization nor a sampled grid.
It passes: the low-side margin is -1/5000, the conservative detector
floor margin is -827/3000000, and both high-side endpoint numerators
are strictly negative throughout the full delta interval. These
checks confirm the bookkeeping, not the missing analytic estimate.

The original purported "stdlib Fraction" checker also imports NumPy
and SciPy to choose a witness t. Those imports failed in the available
Python environments; this is why the independent exact calculation
was used. Its result does not rely on rerunning the old optimizer.

All no-go assertions above have deliberately explicit scope. A failure
to find a power saving, a finite search of functional-equation chambers,
or the present lack of an applicable spectral formula is not a proof
of impossibility for unrestricted future methods.
