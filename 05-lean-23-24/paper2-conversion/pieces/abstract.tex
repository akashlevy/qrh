We prove that every Dirichlet $L$-function, including the Riemann zeta function, has no zeros in the half-plane $\Rea s>23/24$. This is a special case of OpenAI's \emph{The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis}, which proves the half-plane $\Rea s>11/12$ for every finite-order Hecke $L$-function over $K=\Q(\sqrt{-3})$: the case of the Hecke characters that come from Dirichlet characters, at a fixed value of its parameter. We keep its text and remove what this case does not need. The top level of the argument is rearranged to match a Lean~4 formalization, which proves the theorem using only the standard axioms.
