PROPOSITION. The statement and proof of lem:plain remain valid after replacing
the interval 3/4 <= kappa <= 1 by 13/18 <= kappa <= 1, with the same moment
condition n_1+n_2+6 kappa z <= M and, for kappa<1, the same hypothesis
beta_* <= (1+kappa)/2. Uniformity of the mesh persists on this larger interval.

Proof by auditing each occurrence of kappa in the printed proof.

(1) The prime estimate at lines 12833--12898 uses logarithmic control on
Re s=(1+kappa)/2+e. Its only hypotheses are that this line lies to the right
of beta_* and that the parameter is in a fixed compact subset of (0,1].
The logarithmic-control lemma (1531--1605) is uniform for its parameter
a in [1/2,1]. Here (1+kappa)/2 >=31/36>1/2. The prime-power error remains
O(P^(1/2+epsilon)), and the centrally normalized squared prime product is
O(Z^(kappa z+epsilon)). No use of kappa>=3/4 occurs in this step.

(2) The width-floor calculation at lines 12929--12939 prints
z <= M/(6 kappa) <= 2M/9 and kappa z <= M/6.
For the enlarged interval replace 2M/9 by 3M/13. The bound actually used
in the terminal exponent is kappa z <= M/6, unchanged. Thus the terminal
error rho/6 and all subsequent loss allocations remain unchanged.

(3) At lines 12947--12951 the centered comparison has A>5M/6 and
(6 kappa-1)z <= M-A < M/6. The original proof uses 6 kappa-1>=7/2 to
deduce z<M/21<M/20. On the enlarged interval use 6 kappa-1>=10/3
instead, giving z<M/20 directly. In particular A-z>2(M/4).
The following reflection calculation uses only z<M/20:
  A_comp <= 3M/2-A+2z+xi <= 23M/30+xi,
  A_comp+(6 kappa-1)z <= 5M/2-2A+2z+xi <= 14M/15+xi.
Consequently both comparison margins remain at least M/15 before xi,
and the original requirement xi<=rho/30 is sufficient without change.

(4) The child affine defect, lines 14219--14236, only uses nonnegativity
of 6 kappa-1 to pass from z'<=z and erase masks. This remains true.
The greedy slot removal at 14255--14276 divides its defect by 6 kappa;

since kappa<=1. There is no lower-bound use of 3/4 here.

(5) The uniform affine error bound at lines 14822--14829 uses only

These observations cover all kappa occurrences in the proof. QED.

SELECTED ROW COUNTS FOR 13/18 <= kappa <= 3/4.

Write c=1/(6 kappa), so 2/9<=c<=3/13, and alpha=5/6. With the same
amplitude x=q/delta in [0,1/2], the two short-witness count exponents are
  A_I(r)=1-delta[x+(1-x)r],
  S_t(r)=1-delta[2cx+(2-4cx)(t-r)].
Here 1<=t<=3/2. They cross at
  D=3-(1+4c)x,
  r_*(t)=((2-4cx)t+(2c-1)x)/D.
The bounds needed for every selected moment and the prime supply follow
without any new analytic estimate:
  D>=53/26>0,
  r_*(3/2)=1,
  r_*(1)>= (3-2c)/(5-4c) >=23/37,
  1/3 <= t-r_*(t) <= 1/2.
For the first lower bound, r_*(1) decreases with x, while its value at
x=1/2 increases with c. For the second, (t-r_*(t)) increases with t;
at t=1 it is (1-2cx)/D, increasing with x, hence at least 1/3.
Thus inverse capacity remains <=7/37, its second marked-width margin
2r-1 remains >=9/37, and plain capacity is <=c/3<=1/13<7/37. Existing
physical supply ell/h>1/5 therefore suffices with the same rounding margins.

The crossing exponent is
  R_short=1-delta+delta P/D (3/2-t),
  P=(2-4cx)(1-x).
The long-witness exponent remains
  L=1-delta+(alpha-delta)(t-1).
They balance at
  J=(alpha-delta)D+delta P,
  t=1+delta P/(2J),
  R_*=1-delta+(alpha-delta)delta P/(2J).
For delta in [0,alpha], P>=10/13>0 and D>P, so J is bounded away from
zero, t in [1,3/2], and all compactness, rounding, and height arguments
of the original row-count proof persist. If kappa is fixed from an
already established upper bound B on beta_* via kappa=2B-1, the plain
moment hypothesis holds and there is no Delta/4 replacement penalty:
the capacity c is the actual capacity throughout this proof.

APPLICATION TO A STRICTLY SMALLER BOUND.

Given an established B with 31/36<=B<=7/8, this extension allows the
fourth-moment capacity c=1/[6(2B-1)], slightly larger than 2/9 when
B<7/8. To turn this into a theorem, independently certify the low/high
exponent inequalities at a new target theta<B, along with the Euler,
prime-supply, floor, intermediate-row and tail margins. The positive gap
beta_*-theta under contradiction then absorbs all adjustable losses.
This note supplies the previously unstated analytic input needed for that
bootstrap; it does not by itself supply the new endpoint certificate.

LOW-SIDE GENERALIZATION AT BALANCED TOTAL LENGTH.

The source's compensated row-norm statement is tied to ell=1/6, so it
must be reproved from its proof when ell increases. This is harmless.
Put M=1-ell, M'=M-2d, ell'=ell-d, 0<=d<=ell. The source proof at
lines 8240--8269 gives T_d<=H-3d+o(1) without requiring ell=1/6.
Thus max(H,v+ell_b)=H+o(1). The two branches at lines 8270--8324 give
  E_ref <= M' + ((1+3ell'-2M')/4)_+ + o(1)
          = M' + ((5ell-1+d)/4)_+ + o(1).
One must retain this extra term, rather than claim the row norm is
Z^(M'+epsilon) for every subset. Summing rescaled tuples as in the
source's low estimate gives the additional exponent
  f(d)=-d+((5ell-1+d)/8)_+.
If ell<=1/5, f(0)=0 and both linear slopes are negative (-1 and -7/8).
Therefore f(d)<=0 throughout the entire subset interval, exactly and
without a sampled grid. The physical modified probe consequently satisfies
  |I_modified(Z)| << Z^(l_x/2+b/12+epsilon),
provided b=l_y-l_x>0, M=l_x+l_y=1-ell,
l_x>ell, l_y>ell, M>2ell, and l_y-ell-11b/6>0.
Its signal exponent is C(s)=s-(4+b)/6. Matching low and signal gives
  theta_low=11/12-ell/4.
These are direct consequences of the original reflected-energy and Gram
lemmas, not an assumption of a strengthened theta-energy wall.

EULER AND SMALL-ROW EXTENSION NEAR 7/8.

For any target theta in [437/500,7/8], the second Euler region can use
x_r>=theta in place of x_r>=7/8, while keeping z_r>=33/200 and
w_r>=19/20. Its denominators remain uniformly nonzero. The good-prime
defect exponents 4-6x_r-6z_r and 1-x_r-w_r-6z_r are strictly below -1;
the finite ramified factors remain subpower. The four principal local
approximation exponents -x_r, -6z_r, 4-5x_r-6z_r, 1-w_r-6z_r are all
<=-theta throughout this range. Hence B_p=-1+O(p^(-theta)), the
principal normalizer is unchanged, and its approximation margin can be
any 0<kappa_P<theta min_i ell_i.

The shared contour lemmas formally state sigma0>=7/8, but their proofs
use this only to stay in the declared second Euler region and, on the
small-row line, to choose a positive first-region epsilon0. Replace the
former region as above and the latter epsilon0=3/8 by any fixed positive
epsilon0<=theta-1/2. The small-row local bounds G_p=O(1) off the row
and G_p<<p^(1/2) on the row remain valid. The explicit exponent check is
  h(17/50-1/6)-l_y/2+(63/50)d_min<0,
or the slightly stronger conservative version with 2d_min. Principal
contour margins remain l_y/20>0 and h/600>0. All new geometry-dependent
quantities must be checked before copying the final parameter-order proof.

LIMITATIONS OF OLDER BARRIER CLAIMS.

The old 0.874957... LP barrier uses c=2/9, so it does not apply unchanged
to the larger capacity just justified. A coefficient-model lower bound is
a limitation of those particular sufficient estimates, not a lower bound
on the actual L-function zeros. The separate 13/15 calculation survives
as a barrier for the stated low-side majorant together with its floor-bin
absolute-count treatment; it does not prove that arbitrary new treatments
of the probe or all future mean-value estimates are impossible.
